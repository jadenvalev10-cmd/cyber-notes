# TryHackMe: The CIA Triad

Pre Security path, Attacks and Defenses module. October 2026.

**Situation:** Introductory room (Easy, about 30 minutes) on Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability as the core security mindset.

**Task:** Understand what is being protected in cyber security, then classify nine security incidents by which CIA pillar they affect most.

**Action:**

- Worked through the definitions of each pillar.
- Task on the security mindset: for any incident, ask whether data was exposed, whether it was changed without permission, or whether a service became unavailable.
- Completed a drag-and-drop exercise sorting incidents into the right pillar.

**Result:** Completed the room.

**Key terms**

Confidentiality (information not available to unauthorised people), Integrity (information not modified without permission), Availability (information and systems accessible when needed)
