# TryHackMe: Computer Types

Completed.

**Situation:** Needed to understand the different types of computers interacted with day to day, both direct (laptops, phones) and indirect (servers, embedded systems).

**Task:** Identify and distinguish between the different computer types and explain what makes each one suited to its purpose.

**Action:** Sorted computers into two groups.

- **Direct-use devices:**
  - Laptop: portable everyday computing
  - Desktop: sustained performance at a fixed location
  - Workstation: precision and reliability for professional tasks
  - Smartphone: pocket-sized and optimized for battery and connectivity (iPhone, Android)
  - Tablet: touch-first with a larger screen (iPad, drawing tablet)
- **Behind-the-scenes devices:**
  - Server: no screen or keyboard; serves many users over a network
  - IoT device: single-purpose and network-connected (thermostat, smart doorbell, fitness tracker)
  - Embedded computer: built into another device (coffee maker controller, automatic door sensor, lamp dimmer chip)

**Result:** A framework for sorting any computer by purpose and design tradeoffs. Direct-interaction devices are built for flexibility; background devices are built to do one job reliably without a screen.
